Career timeline

Milestones that shaped the numbers.

2007
International debut

ODI debut against Ireland in Belfast and T20I debut at the inaugural ICC World T20.

2010
Maiden ODI century

114 against Zimbabwe — the first of 33 ODI hundreds.

2013
Test debut & first ODI double

177 on Test debut against the West Indies in Kolkata, then 209 against Australia in Bengaluru — his first ODI double century.

2014
World record 264

The highest individual score in ODI history, against Sri Lanka at Eden Gardens.

2017
Maiden T20I century

118 against Sri Lanka in Indore — one of the fastest T20I hundreds at the time.

2019
World Cup record haul

648 runs with a record seven centuries in a single World Cup, earning ICC ODI Cricketer of the Year.

2024
T20 World Cup winning captain

Captained India to the ICC Men's T20 World Cup title, then retired from T20 internationals.

2025
Champions Trophy & Test farewell

Led India to the ICC Champions Trophy title and retired from Test cricket.

At a glance

Career summary

Rohit Sharma is the only player to score three double centuries in ODI cricket and holds the record for the highest individual score in the format. He finished his T20I career as the format's leading run-scorer and six-hitter, and captained Mumbai Indians to five IPL titles.

ODI debut June 2007
Test debut November 2013
T20I debut September 2007
IPL franchise Mumbai Indians
Batting style Right-hand top order
